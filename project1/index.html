My Favorite Movie: Inception


Basic Information About the Movie

Inception is a 2010 science fiction action film written and directed by Christopher Nolan. It is based on the idea of dreaming within a dream.

Main Cast

  • Leonardo DiCaprio (Cobb)
  • Joseph Gordon-Levitt (Arthur)
  • Elliot Page (Ariadne)
  • Tom Hardy (Eames)

My Top 3 Favorite Scenes

  2. The scene where the city folds onto itself.
  3. The spinning top scene at the end of the movie.
